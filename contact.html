FAQ

Before you get in touch

Do you work online?

Yes. Consultations are available online via Zoom.

Who do you work with?

Musicians, music industry professionals and creative individuals, plus organisations, educators and institutions who want consultancy, psychoeducation or CPD.

Are your therapists accredited?

Our psychotherapists are UKCP registered or accredited, and our psychiatrist is a member of the Royal College of Psychiatrists. See the team for full qualifications.

What if I need urgent help?

Music & Mind is not an emergency service. Call 999 in an emergency, contact NHS 111, or call Samaritans free on 116 123.
