About us

An independent team for the music world's mental health

Music & Mind is an independent team of experienced psychotherapists and mental health professionals specialising in the psychological wellbeing of musicians, music industry professionals and creative individuals.

Our story

Built by people who've lived it

Music & Mind was founded by Dr Adam Ficek, who brings a background in the music industry as a performer, educator, researcher and clinician. The aim is simple: to offer effective, informed support and resources within the demanding environments that performers, creatives and music industry personnel work in.

Alongside one-to-one therapy, the team delivers psychoeducation, consultancy and research, and contributes extensively to media on musicians, creatives and mental health.

CB

Dr Claire Bloxsom

Psychotherapist

CertHE. BSc. Clin Dip. Clin Dip. MSc. PhD. CPsychol. AFBPsS. FHEA.

Dr Claire Bloxsom is a highly experienced trainer in psychology and mental health, involved in university-level teaching and research since 2004. She runs her own private clinical psychotherapy practice as a UKCP registered and accredited integrative psychotherapist.

Clinically, Claire is passionate about the creative arts and the influence of music on wellbeing and mental health within the occupational environment, and has extensive experience supporting clients from the gay, bisexual, queer and lesbian communities. She is a singer and composer involved in independently released music projects, a PRS member, and has a personal understanding of the positive effects of musicking for psychological wellbeing.

Our approach

Specialist, integrative and informed by research

Integrative therapy

We draw on a range of recognised approaches, including integrative psychotherapy, EMDR and sensorimotor psychotherapy, tailored to the individual.

Evidence and research

Our clinicians are active researchers. Our work is grounded in studies of professional musicians' experiences of pressure, pride, insecurity and identity.

Professional standards

Accredited and insured, working within the UKCP's SCoPEd framework and the standards of their professional bodies.
