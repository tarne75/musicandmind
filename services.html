Services

Specialist support for life and work in music

Music & Mind provides specialist psychotherapy and psychological support for musicians and music industry professionals, alongside consultancy, psychoeducation, CPD and educational services for individuals, organisations and professionals working in music and the creative industries.

Who we work with

Performers, songwriters, producers, DJs, crew, managers, label and industry staff, educators, and anyone building a life around music.

How we work

Confidential, non-judgemental consultations held online via Zoom, shaped around your needs and the realities of your schedule.

What we cover

A range of psychological and mental health issues, as well as the wider psychological pressures associated with working in music.
