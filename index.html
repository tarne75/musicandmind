What we do

Therapy, education and consultancy under one roof

Music & Mind is an independent team of psychotherapists and mental health professionals focused on the psychological wellbeing of musicians, music industry professionals and creatives.

Psychotherapy & support

Confidential one-to-one therapy for the psychological pressures of working in music, from performance anxiety to burnout, addiction, identity and relationships.

See what we support

Psychoeducation & CPD

Talks, workshops and training that help musicians, educators and managers understand mental health and build healthier ways of working.

Learn about CPD

Consultancy & research

Psychological consultation, research and content development for organisations, charities, universities and businesses in the creative industries.

Discuss a project

Three simple steps
